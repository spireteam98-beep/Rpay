## Public-claims guardrails

- Describe licenses, cards, safeguarding and SEPA access as planned or in development until formally issued.
- Use "designed for" or "compliance-ready architecture" for regulatory capabilities.
- Avoid publishing confidential financial projections, founder phone numbers, personal immigration details or ownership structure.
- Use sourced market figures as category context, not guaranteed company performance.
