Cookie Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

This policy explains how wayaki.com and the Wayaki web app use cookies and similar technologies, including browser storage.

What we currently use

  • Essential storage: used to keep you signed in, remember security state, and preserve necessary app preferences.
  • Preference storage: used to remember settings you choose on your device.

Wayaki does not currently use advertising cookies or sell information collected through cookies. If analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before they are used.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and site data through your browser settings. Clearing essential storage signs you out and may reset preferences. Mobile operating systems also let you remove locally stored app information by deleting the app.

Third-party services

Some features connect to service providers. Their websites or hosted interfaces may apply their own cookie policies when you visit them directly.
